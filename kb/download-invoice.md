# Download a monthly subscription invoice

Practice admins can download invoices for the practice's subscription.

1. Go to **Settings > Billing > Invoices**.
2. Find the month you need and select **Download PDF**.

Invoices are issued on the first day of each month and stay available for seven years. To have invoices emailed automatically, add a billing contact under **Settings > Billing > Contacts**. Only users with the practice admin role can see the Billing section.
