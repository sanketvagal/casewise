# Reschedule a patient appointment

Front-desk staff can move an existing appointment without cancelling it.

1. Open **Schedule** and find the appointment on the calendar.
2. Select the appointment, then choose **Reschedule**.
3. Pick a new date, time and provider, then select **Save**.

The patient receives an updated confirmation by their preferred contact method. The original slot is released immediately so it can be booked by someone else. Rescheduling keeps the appointment's notes and visit reason.
