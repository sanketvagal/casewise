# Reset a staff member's password

Staff can reset their own password from the sign-in page.

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the work email address linked to the account.
3. Open the reset email (it arrives within 5 minutes and the link expires after 60 minutes) and choose a new password.

Passwords must be at least 12 characters. If the reset email does not arrive, check the spam folder, then ask a practice admin to confirm the email address under **Settings > Staff**. A practice admin can also send a reset link from the staff member's profile.
