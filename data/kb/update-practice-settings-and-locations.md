# Update practice details, locations, and provider identifiers

To keep your billing and demographics accurate, administrators can update practice profiles, service locations, and provider identifiers at any time.

### Steps to Update Practice Information
1. Navigate to **Admin > Practice Setup**.
2. Select the **Details & Tax ID** tab to modify your legal business name, physical address, or federal Tax ID.
3. Open the **Locations** tab to add or edit physical service addresses and assign unique location NPI numbers.
4. Go to **Admin > Providers**, click a staff member's name, and update their individual NPI and taxonomy codes in the **Billing Identifiers** section.
5. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of each page.

### Limits and Conditions
Only users with the Administrator role can modify practice-level settings and tax identifiers. Changes apply immediately to new drafts, but do not automatically retroactively update claims already generated or queued.

### Troubleshooting
If updated practice information does not appear on outgoing claims, verify that the specific billing location is correctly linked under **Billing > Claim Defaults**. If the issue persists, clear your browser cache or contact support with the affected claim ID.
