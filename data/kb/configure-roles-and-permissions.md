# Assign user roles and configure permission levels

Protect sensitive clinical and financial data by tailoring access for your staff. Only practice managers and primary administrators can modify security credentials.

1. Go to **Settings > Practice Staff > Roles & Permissions**.
2. Click **Create New Role** or select an existing title from the directory.
3. Toggle permissions across categories, including *Billing*, *Patient Charts*, and *System Settings*.
4. Click **Assign Users**, select the staff members who require this profile, and click **Save Changes**.

**Limits & Conditions:** Every active user profile must be assigned to at least one role. Direct permission overrides on individual accounts are not permitted; all restrictions must flow through the assigned role.

**Troubleshooting:** If a staff member cannot view a restricted menu after saving, verify their account status is active and have them clear their browser cache before logging back in.
