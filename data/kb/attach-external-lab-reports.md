# Upload and attach external paper lab reports to charts

To integrate outside paper reports or external PDF imaging into a patient's record, clinic staff must follow the secure ingestion workflow.

### Steps to Upload
1. Scan the paper document or save the external file as a standard PDF. Ensure the file size does not exceed 25 MB.
2. Navigate to **Patients > Chart Search** and locate the correct individual.
3. Open the chart and go to **Documents > Lab Reports**.
4. Click **Upload New**, select your file, and choose the matching lab order from the dropdown list to link them.
5. Click **Save and Verify** to complete the attachment.

### Limits & Conditions
Only PDF, JPG, and PNG formats are accepted. Files over 25 MB will fail to process.

### Troubleshooting
If the upload fails or the target lab order does not appear in the dropdown, verify that the lab order was previously created in **Labs > Active Orders**. For persistent upload errors, compress your PDF using the internal document toolkit or contact IT support with the error code.
