# Post payments and insurance remittances to accounts

Recording patient payments and electronic remittance advices (ERAs) ensures accurate ledger balances. Only users with the Billing Specialist role can apply or adjust remittance entries.

1. Go to **Billing > Payment Processing**.
2. Select the **Unposted Remittances** tab and choose the desired ERA or paper check batch.
3. Click **Match Invoices** to auto-allocate funds, or manually enter amounts against specific service lines.
4. Review the remaining balance and click **Post & Close** to finalize.

**Limits:** Adjustments cannot exceed the original invoice line balance. Fully closed billing periods will reject new postings.

**Troubleshooting:** If a payment is misapplied to the wrong invoice line, navigate to **Billing > Ledger History**, locate the transaction, and click **Reverse Allocation**. You can then reapply the funds using the steps above.
