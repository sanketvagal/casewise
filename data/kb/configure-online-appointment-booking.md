# Set up and manage online appointment booking for patients

Enable patients to schedule routine visits directly through the web and mobile portal by configuring your scheduling rules. Only providers with active schedules and visit types marked as 'Online Eligible' will appear to patients.

1. Go to **Settings > Patient Portal > Booking Rules**.
2. Toggle on **Allow Online Appointment Booking**.
3. Navigate to **Scheduling > Visit Types**, select a visit type, and check the box for **Available Online**.
4. Under **Settings > Schedule > Provider Availability**, designate which staff members accept online requests.

**Limits & Conditions:**
Patients can only book appointments between 24 hours and 60 days in advance. Urgent visit types cannot be made available online.

**Troubleshooting:**
If a provider does not appear for booking, verify their schedule template is published and that their assigned rooms are active under **Settings > Facility > Rooms**.
