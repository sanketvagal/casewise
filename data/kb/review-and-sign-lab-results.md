# Review, sign off, and annotate incoming lab results

Physicians can review, annotate, and sign off on incoming diagnostics efficiently by following the steps below. Only users with clinical provider roles may sign results.

1. Navigate to **Clinical > Lab Queue**.
2. Select the pending result from the unreviewed list to open the viewer.
3. Type any necessary clinical notes into the **Annotations** text box.
4. To route an abnormal finding to a covering physician, click **Delegate**, select the covering provider from the dropdown, and click **Send**.
5. Click **Sign Off** to finalize the review and automatically file the report into the patient chart.

**Limits & Conditions**
Results marked critical cannot be signed off without an added annotation. Delegated results remain locked for the original ordering physician until the covering provider completes the review.

**Troubleshooting**
If the **Sign Off** button appears grayed out, verify that all mandatory annotation fields are complete. If a covering provider does not appear in the delegation list, check **Settings > Staff** to ensure their active coverage schedule is correctly configured.
