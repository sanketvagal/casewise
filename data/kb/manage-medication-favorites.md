# Create and manage personal medication favorites

Save time during e-prescribing by building a personalized list of frequently ordered medications and instructions.

### How to Create a Favorite
1. Go to **Patients > Chart** and open the e-prescribing module.
2. Search for and select your desired medication and dosage.
3. Enter the preferred sig in the directions field.
4. Click **Save to Favorites** in the action toolbar.
5. Assign a custom label and click **Confirm**.

### Limits and Conditions
* Each clinician can store up to 150 items in their personal favorites list.
* Shared clinic-wide favorites are managed separately through **Settings > E-Prescribing > Practice Defaults**.

### Troubleshooting
If your favorites disappear following a software update, navigate to **Tools > Preferences > Restore Defaults** and select **Reload Personal Prescribing Lists**. If the list remains empty after reloading, contact the IT help desk to verify your user profile permissions.
