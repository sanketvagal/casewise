# Build custom lab order panels and requisition bundles

Streamline frequent lab orders by grouping multiple tests into a single reusable panel. Only users with clinical administrator roles can create and publish shared panels for the practice.

1. Navigate to **Clinical Setup > Lab Management > Order Panels**.
2. Click **Create New Panel** in the top right corner.
3. Enter a descriptive **Panel Name** and select the applicable clinical specialty.
4. Use the search bar to locate and add individual tests from the master catalog.
5. Click **Save and Publish** to make the bundle available to your provider group.

### Limits and Conditions
* Panels are limited to a maximum of 25 individual tests.
* Custom panels cannot override specific external reference laboratory coding requirements.

### Troubleshooting
If providers cannot view the new panel in their order menu, verify that the panel's sharing permissions are assigned to their specific department under **Settings > Permissions > Lab Access**.
