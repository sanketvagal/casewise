# Set up a series of recurring appointments

To book regular visits for a patient requiring ongoing care, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to **Scheduling > Calendar** and select the patient's initial time slot.
2. Click **New Appointment**, complete the visit details, and toggle **Recurring Series** to **On**.
3. Choose the frequency (e.g., weekly or monthly), set the end date or total number of occurrences, and click **Save Series**.

### Conditions and Limits
Recurring series can be scheduled up to 12 months in advance. A maximum of 52 occurrences can be generated in a single series.

### Troubleshooting Conflicts
If the system detects a scheduling conflict (such as a provider vacation, holiday closure, or an already booked time slot), a conflict resolution prompt appears. You can choose to skip the conflicting date, assign the visit to a covering provider, or manually select a new time slot for that specific occurrence before finalizing the rest of the series. If the entire series fails to generate, verify that the selected appointment type is permitted for recurring bookings under **Settings > Appointment Types**.
