# Update payment methods for practice subscription invoices

Practice managers can update the credit card used for monthly software subscription fees and review past billing statements directly within the account portal. 

### Update Payment Details
1. Navigate to **Billing > Subscription Settings**.
2. Select the **Payment Methods** tab.
3. Click **Add New Card** or select **Edit** next to your current card.
4. Enter the updated billing address and card details, then click **Save Changes**.

### Review Invoices
1. Go to **Billing > Invoice History**.
2. Click any invoice number to download the PDF statement.

### Limits and Conditions
* Only users with the Practice Manager role can access subscription billing settings.
* Accepted payment methods include Visa, Mastercard, and American Express.
* Changes take effect immediately for the next billing cycle.

### Troubleshooting
If a payment update fails, verify that the billing ZIP code matches your bank records exactly. If charges are declined despite correct details, contact your financial institution to authorize recurring software subscription charges before trying again in **Billing > Subscription Settings**.
