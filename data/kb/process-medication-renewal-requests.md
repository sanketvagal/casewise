# Review and process pharmacy medication renewal requests

Electronic renewal requests sent from pharmacies require prompt clinical review. To process these submissions and link them to patient charts, follow the steps below.

1. Go to **Clinical > Pharmacy Queue**.
2. Select the **Renewal Requests** tab to view pending items.
3. Click the patient's name to open the request and review the proposed medication against their active chart.
4. Click **Approve**, **Deny**, or **Modify**. Approving automatically updates the patient's active medication list.

### Limits and Conditions
* Only users with prescribing or clinical oversight roles can process renewals.
* Requests expire automatically after 14 days if left unaddressed.

### Troubleshooting
If an approved renewal does not appear on the active medication list, verify that the pharmacy transmission completed successfully in **Reports > Audit Trail**. If the status shows an error, manually add the medication via **Charts > Medications** and contact support if the queue remains out of sync.
