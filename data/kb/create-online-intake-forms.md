# Create and publish digital intake forms for patients

Custom intake questionnaires streamline the check-in process by letting patients submit information securely before arrival.

### Steps to Create and Publish a Form
1. Go to **Patient Portal > Form Builder**.
2. Click **New Form** and select a template type, such as *Medical History* or *Consent*.
3. Drag and drop desired fields into the layout area. Required fields must have the **Mandatory** toggle enabled.
4. Click **Save Draft**, then select **Publish to Portal**.
5. Navigate to **Patient Portal > Settings** to assign the form to specific appointment types.

### Limits and Conditions
- Forms can only be published if all required field validation checks pass without errors.
- Patients must have an active portal account to receive and complete assigned questionnaires.
- Maximum file upload size within a single form is 25 MB.

### Troubleshooting
If form fields trigger validation errors upon publication, check for duplicate field IDs or unassigned dropdown options. Clear your browser cache and reload **Form Builder** if changes do not appear on the live patient portal immediately. Contact support if validation errors persist after correcting all flagged items.
