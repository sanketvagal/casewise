# Update provider working hours and calendar blocks

To keep appointment slots accurate, you can update regular working hours and add one-time calendar blocks for meetings or time off. 

1. Go to **Settings > Scheduling > Provider Hours**.
2. Select the target clinician from the **Provider** dropdown menu.
3. Adjust the weekly schedule by entering start and end times, or check **Day Off** for non-working days.
4. To block specific dates, click **Add Block**, choose your start and end timestamps, and select a reason from the category list.
5. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the page.

### Limits and Conditions
Changes apply immediately to future scheduling slots. Past appointments remain unaffected, and you cannot overlap a working-hours shift with an existing confirmed appointment.

### Troubleshooting
If the calendar still displays incorrect availability, clear your browser cache and verify that no conflicting group-schedule overrides are active under **Settings > Scheduling > Facility Hours**. If issues persist, ensure the provider's primary time zone matches your clinic's main location setting.
