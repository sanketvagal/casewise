# Process online patient payments through the portal

Patients can securely pay copays and outstanding balances online before or after their visits. Staff can review these transactions in real time.

### Steps to Process and Verify Payments
1. Go to **Billing > Portal Transactions** to view incoming online payments.
2. Locate the patient's name and check the status column for confirmation.
3. Click **Process Receipt** to post the funds directly to the patient ledger.

### Limits and Conditions
- The maximum single online payment is $2,500.00.
- Accepted payment methods include Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and HSA/FSA debit cards.
- Transactions initiated after 8:00 PM EST post to the ledger on the next business day.

### Troubleshooting
If a patient reports a card decline, verify that their billing address matches the card issuer's file exactly. For persistent gateway errors, navigate to **Settings > Payment Gateway** and click **Test Connection** to reset the merchant API sync. If the gateway remains offline, contact merchant support and instruct the patient to pay via the front desk terminal.
